About Terre Haute Help

A free, independent directory of community resources for people in Terre Haute and Vigo County, Indiana.

What this site is

Terre Haute Help lists local services in one place: food pantries and meals, shelter and housing help, healthcare, crisis support, legal aid, transportation, and more. Each listing gives the practical details you need to make a call or a visit, such as what the service offers, how to reach it, its hours, and what to bring.

The site is free to use. There is nothing to sign up for, no account, and no advertising. The services listed are separate, and some have their own costs or requirements.

What it is not

Terre Haute Help does not run any of the services listed here. Each organization decides its own services, eligibility, hours, and availability, and those can change without notice. A listing is not a promise that a program is open, has space, or will be able to help you, so please call the provider before you travel.

If you need help right now, see the crisis and emergency lines. For emergencies, call 911.

Why I built it

I've been where many people using this site are now: hopeless, hungry, with nowhere to lay my head at night, and with little to no help or support. I wish I'd had something like this back then.

When someone in Terre Haute needs help, the information is scattered across dozens of websites, outdated pages, and word of mouth. Finding it shouldn't be that hard, especially in a crisis. I built Terre Haute Help for the people who are in that place right now.

Who runs it

My name is Robert Casey. I live in Terre Haute and study computer science at Ivy Tech Community College. I created Terre Haute Help and am responsible for it.

The site is independent. It is not affiliated with any government agency, political organization, or business. It has no ads, no paywalls, and no user accounts; the privacy policy explains what the web server records.

How listings are researched and updated

Resource information is researched using official websites, publicly available information, and direct contact with providers when possible. Some information also comes from firsthand experience: I have used some of these services myself, including a food pantry. I have not visited or contacted every organization listed.

Each listing shows the month it was last checked, and listings are reviewed as new information becomes available. Services, hours, eligibility requirements, and availability can change, so please confirm important details directly with the organization before traveling.

Corrections and suggestions

If you find information that is wrong or out of date, or you know of a local resource that should be listed, email terrehautehelp@gmail.com. It helps to include the name of the listing and what has changed.

Organizations listed on the site can use the same address to update their information, correct an error, or ask to be removed.
