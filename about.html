Why this site exists

When someone in Terre Haute needs help, the information they need is scattered across dozens of websites, outdated government pages, and word of mouth. Food pantries, shelters, free clinics, legal aid, crisis lines. Finding it fast, especially in a moment of crisis, shouldn't be that hard.

Terre Haute Help exists to fix that. One place, plain language, verified information, free on any device. If you need help, you shouldn't have to work to find it.

Every listing is reviewed and includes addresses, phone numbers, hours, and what to bring. The practical details that matter when you're actually trying to access a service.

I've been where you are. Hopeless, hungry, nowhere to lay my head at night, with little to no help or support. I wish I'd had something like this back then. That's why I built it, for the people who are in that place right now. If you find an error or need help finding the right resource, email us at terrehautehelp@gmail.com.

Who built this

My name is Robert Casey. I'm a Terre Haute resident and CS student at Ivy Tech Community College. I built this site because access to basic community resources shouldn't depend on knowing the right people or navigating a maze of outdated information.

Terre Haute has a lot of people who need help and a lot of organizations trying to provide it. The gap between them is often just a lack of clear, accessible information. This site is an attempt to close that gap.

Everything here was built and is maintained by hand. Researching organizations, verifying contact information, and keeping listings current. If you find something out of date or know of a resource that should be listed, I want to hear from you.

How the directory works

Every resource listed on this site was individually researched and verified. Listings include the organization's name, a plain-language description of what they offer, address, phone number, hours, and what to bring or expect when you contact them.

Listings are tagged with a last-verified date. When information changes, we update it. If you notice something wrong, outdated, or missing, let us know. This directory is only as useful as it is accurate.

This site has no ads, no paywalls, no user accounts, and no tracking. It is not affiliated with any government agency, political organization, or commercial entity. It is a free public resource maintained by volunteers.

How to help

The most useful thing you can do is share this site with someone who might need it. If you work at or know of a local organization that should be listed, or if you notice information that needs updating, email us.

If you are an organization listed here and want to update your information, correct an error, or be removed, contact us and we will take care of it promptly.
