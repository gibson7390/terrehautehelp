Terre Haute Bus Routes

All 10 city bus routes, hours, and how to ride — pulled directly from the official Terre Haute Transit Utility. No car needed to get to food, healthcare, or services.

🚌

Terre Haute Transit is free for all riders as of January 1, 2025. No fare is required for any fixed route or ADA paratransit trip. Just get on the bus.

⚠

Bus schedules change. Always confirm before you ride at the official THTU website or call 812-235-0109. No Sunday service. Service suspended on major holidays.

ADA Paratransit — Wheels to the World

Door-to-door transportation is available for residents who qualify under the Americans with Disabilities Act. This service is also free and runs during the same hours as fixed routes — Monday through Saturday, 6AM to 10PM.

How to book: Call at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day rides are not guaranteed.

Eligibility: Must have a disability that prevents you from using the fixed-route buses. Call to apply for eligibility certification.

Phone: 812-235-0109 · Office: 901 S. 14th Street, Terre Haute, IN 47807

About this site

Terre Haute Help is a free, independent resource directory maintained by local volunteers. All listings are verified and updated regularly. This site has no ads, no paywalls, and no affiliation with any government agency.

To report an incorrect or outdated listing, email terrehautehelp@gmail.com.

Built by neighbors, for neighbors

This site was created by Robert Casey to help ensure that individuals in Vigo County can easily find the support and resources they need.
