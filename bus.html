Before you ride

Fare

Terre Haute Transit has been fare free for all riders since January 1, 2025.

Catching a city bus

  • Be at your stop at least 10 minutes before the bus is due.
  • Signal the driver one block before your stop.

Service days

Monday–Saturday. No Sunday service, and no service on several holidays: see the holiday schedule (opens in a new tab).

Transfer Center

750 Cherry Street, at 8th and Cherry Streets, downtown Terre Haute, IN 47807. Buses meet here; it is not the Transit Utility's office.

ADA paratransit: Wheels to the World

Wheels to the World is Terre Haute Transit's ADA service for people who, because of a disability, cannot use the regular fixed-route buses. It is not a bus you can board at a stop: riders must be approved first, and each trip is arranged by phone. It runs during the same hours as the fixed-route buses.

Service area: trips must start and end within 3/4 mile of a fixed bus route, and only while that route is running. Riders who live farther away can still use the service for trips that start and end inside that area.

  1. 1Step 1: Apply for eligibility. The application is on Terre Haute Transit's ADA and accessibility page (opens in a new tab). A health care professional completes part of it. For help, call 812-235-0109.
  2. 2Step 2: Book each trip by phone once approved. Call 812-235-0109 at least one day before your trip. Terre Haute Transit's rider guide says reservations are taken Monday–Saturday, 6 AM–10 PM, up to 14 days ahead, and that same-day trips are not guaranteed.

Riders with disabilities who use the fixed-route buses can also ask for a change to how a trip is handled. Call 812-235-0109 at least the day before the trip.

About this site

Terre Haute Help is a free, independent resource directory. Information is researched using official websites, public information, and direct contact with providers when possible. Programs, hours, eligibility, and availability can change, so confirm details with the provider before traveling and report outdated information to us. This site has no ads, no paywalls, and no affiliation with any government agency.

To report an incorrect or outdated listing, email terrehautehelp@gmail.com.

Built by neighbors, for neighbors

This site was created by Robert Casey to help ensure that individuals in Vigo County can easily find the support and resources they need.
