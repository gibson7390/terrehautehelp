About this site

Terre Haute Help is a free, independent resource directory. Information is researched using official websites, public information, and direct contact with providers when possible. Programs, hours, eligibility, and availability can change, so confirm details with the provider before traveling and report outdated information to us. This site has no ads, no paywalls, and no affiliation with any government agency.

To report an incorrect or outdated listing, email terrehautehelp@gmail.com.

Built by neighbors, for neighbors

This site was created by Robert Casey to help ensure that individuals in Vigo County can easily find the support and resources they need.
