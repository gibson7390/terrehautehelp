1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the data practices of Terre Haute Help, the website located at https://terrehautehelp.org (the "Site"). The Site is a free, purely informational public directory of local community resources for residents of Terre Haute and Vigo County, Indiana. It is operated independently by Robert Casey.

The Site is designed as a resource-only directory with no user accounts and no forms for visitors to submit information, so its data practices are limited. This Policy explains what occurs — and what does not occur — when you visit the Site.

By accessing or using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described herein.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name or contact details. The only information recorded about a visit is the technical request data described in Sections 3 and 4. Specifically:

  • The Site has no contact forms, sign-up options, accounts, newsletters, donation buttons, or comment sections. Its only input control is the resource search box, described below.
  • No visitor is required to provide a name, email address, phone number, physical address, financial information, or any other personal details to use the Site. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains.
  • We do not use analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media widgets. Two outside services are involved in delivering the Site, Google Fonts and Cloudflare, as described in Section 4.
  • The resource search runs entirely within your browser. What you type is not sent to any server and is not stored by the Site.

3. Server Log Data

Like most websites hosted on a web server, the Site's server records standard access logs for HTTP/HTTPS requests. These logs are created solely by the normal operation of the web server software and may include:

  • The visitor's IP address
  • The date and time of the request
  • The specific URL or resource requested
  • The HTTP method used (GET, HEAD, etc.) and the status code returned
  • The referring URL, if any
  • The user-agent string identifying the browser type and operating system

These logs are used for the technical operation and security of the Site, including detecting and preventing abuse.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose these logs to any third parties for marketing, advertising, or any commercial purpose. How long logs are kept depends on the server's log settings.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site's own pages and scripts do not set cookies or use web beacons, tracking pixels, local storage, fingerprinting, or any other client-side tracking or identification technologies. The outside services described below operate under their own policies.

No analytics or advertising code is loaded. Two outside services are involved in delivering the Site:

  • Google Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so your browser sends Google a standard request, including your IP address and browser information, when a page loads. Google handles this request under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.
  • Cloudflare. The Site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes each request, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the Site. Cloudflare may add a small script that protects email addresses on the page from automated collection, may ask your browser to report network errors, and, depending on its security settings, can set its own cookies. Cloudflare handles this data under its own privacy policy.

5. Third-Party Links and External Resources

The Site consists primarily of curated links to independent third-party organizations, government agencies, hotlines, and public services. When you click any such link, you leave Terre Haute Help entirely and become subject to the privacy policies, data practices, and terms of the external website or organization.

We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy practices, or data collection activities of any third-party site. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of each external resource before providing any information to it.

6. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) to the extent it applies to a purely informational, non-interactive directory.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the server that hosts the Site secure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of the limited server log data described in Section 3.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Server log data is kept according to the server's log settings, as described in Section 3. The Site has no database of visitor information. Upon a valid legal request from a duly authorized law-enforcement or regulatory authority, we will cooperate as required by applicable Indiana or federal law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. Your continued use of the Site after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Indiana and applicable federal laws of the United States, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.
